# DesignWorkBench v2 execution goal

Freeze and validate Memi DesignWorkBench v2 through real practitioner
calibration without treating generated task contracts as completed evidence.

## Completion contract

1. Freeze the exact Memi 2.7 candidate commit and intended claims.
2. Define 15 practitioner tracks, artifact contracts, role rubrics, evidence
   caps, negative controls, and noncompensating scoring.
3. Build 300 legally usable, reproducible tasks with immutable fixtures.
4. Verify browser, iOS, Expo, Android, Figma, motion, spatial/XR, and artifact
   runners against representative tasks.
5. Collect five artifacts from at least four qualified practitioners per track,
   including at least two external practitioners.
6. Obtain two blinded ratings per artifact and adjudicate disagreements above
   eight points.
7. Require reliability of at least 0.80 overall and 0.67 for every track.
8. Freeze the benchmark only after private and holdout material passes an
   independent integrity review.

## Non-negotiable boundary

External practitioner work cannot be synthesized, impersonated, or inferred
from Memi's own outputs. Until that evidence is collected, the benchmark
foundation may be complete while practitioner calibration and the release goal
remain blocked.
